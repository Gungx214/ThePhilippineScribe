COTABATO CITY --- Anti-narcotics agents had seized two kilos of shabu from a Malaysian national and his two local accomplices killed on Sunday in an entrapment operation in Barangay Tubig Mampalam in Bongao, Tawi-Tawi that turned haywire.

Local executives and members of the multi-sector Tawi-Tawi Provincial Peace and Order Council, whose chairperson is Gov. Ysmael Sali, confirmed to reporters on Monday that the incident left the Malaysian Janimal Bin Hajad, Sansibar Amil Amirul and Alma Sarikin Amirul dead.

Agents of the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency-Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao and policemen were about to arrest the three of them and Nurhaipa Ansari Hajim after selling to them shabu at a beachside area in Barangay Mampalam, but they resisted, pulled out guns, provoking a gunfight. 

Hajim yielded voluntarily after her three companions, among them the Malaysian Hajad, were felled with pistol shots by the Tawi-Tawi team of PDEA-BARMM, led by Abraham Solon Kalim.

George Paul Alcovindas, regional director of PDEA-BARMM, told reporters in Cotabato City on Monday that the entrapment operation was laid with the help of the office of Tawi-Tawi Gov. Ysmael Sali and officials of different units in the province of the Police Regional Office-Bangsamoro Autonomous Region and personnel of the Navy's 4th Marine Rifle Battalion. 

He said the Tawi-Tawi PDEA-BARMM group is now in custody of the P13.6 million worth of shabu seized during the entrapment operation.

“It was premised on tips by informants about the large-scale peddling of shabu by those four individuals,” Alcovindas said.

PDEA-BARMM agents in Tawi-Tawi, one of the five provinces in the Bangsamoro region, are to prosecute Hajim, now locked in a detention facility, for violation of the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002.

Alcovindas said they are grateful to municipal officials in Bongao and their provincial government, the Bongao Municipal Police Station and the intelligence agents from the Police Regional Office 9 for having supported the entrapment operation that also resulted in the confiscation of P13.6 million worth of shabu from the four peddlers, all known in Tawi-Tawi for their illegal activities in its island towns.
