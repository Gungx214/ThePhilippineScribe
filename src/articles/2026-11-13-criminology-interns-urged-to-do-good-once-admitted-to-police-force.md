**COTABATO CITY** --- The governor of Cotabato, one of the provinces in Administrative Region 12, on Thursday, November 12, urged criminology interns from the Central Mindanao Colleges (CMC) in Kidapawan City to serve the local communities well once admitted as full-fledged personnel of the state’s police force.

Cotabato Gov. Emmylou Taliño-Mendoza was guest speaker in the College of Criminal Justice Exemplary Culmination Ceremony of Criminology Interns Batch Alab-Agila of the CMC at the provincial capitol in Kidapawan City on Thursday.

She then called on the criminology interns to engage extensively in community service and humanitarian initiatives, besides their law-enforcement duties once they become members of the Philippine National Police.

“It is not all about wearing uniforms and performing police duties. It should also be caring well for the local communities, standing for what is right, getting involved in cross-section activities essential in sustaining peace and understanding among the culturally-pluralistic communities you are soon to serve,” Taliño-Mendoza said.

Thursday’s activity of the CMC was held at the Cotabato provincial capitol gymnasium in Barangay Amas in Kidapawan City. The CMC’s campus is in Kidapawan City, the capital of Cotabato province, which also covers 17 towns that are homes to mixed Muslim, Christian and indigenous non-Moro communities.

Taliño-Mendoza is also chairperson of the influential multi-sector Regional Development Council 12, encompassing the provinces of Cotabato, Sultan Kudarat, South Cotabato and Sarangani and the cities of Kidapawan, Tacurong, Koronadal and General Santos. 

Taliño-Mendoza, now in her second term as governor, is a known supporter of the peacebuilding and community service thrusts in Cotabato province of the Army’s 6th and 10th Infantry Divisions and the Police Regional Office 12, which is based in General Santos City, and its units under the Cotabato Provincial Police Office.

The commander of the 10th ID, Major Gen. Alvin Luzon, and his counterpart in the 6th ID, Major Gen. Edgar Catu, had earlier acknowledged separately Taliño-Mendoza’s role in helping units under them clear, in recent years, all of the towns in the province from presence of the New People’s Army via community-based peacebuilding initiatives and backchannel dialogues supported by her constituent-mayors and barangay officials.
