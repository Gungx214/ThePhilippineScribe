**COTABATO CITY** --- Residents of the Bangsamoro region were sad learning that new officials of their education ministry, controversial long before they came in to improve its operation, found in its warehouse 1,304 android tablets and computers earmarked for schools, never utilized.

Organic personnel of the Ministry of Basic, Higher and Technical Education-Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (MBHTE-BARMM), serving since the Bangsamoro government’s inception in 2019, confirmed on Friday, November 20, that the 1,304 android tablets and computers were intended for schools to boost learning processes for children and hasten their connectivity with teachers during the height of the Covid 19 pandemic, from middle of 2020 to 2023.

The newly-appointed minister of the MBHTE-BARMM, the lawyer Naguib Sinarimbo, also an elected member of the Bangsamoro regional parliament, and his subordinate-regional education officials found on Thursday the devices just piled inside a warehouse, many of which are no longer functional.

Sinarimbo assumed as BARMM’s education minister just a week ago.

Sinarimbo and his co-workers in the MBHTE-BARMM also found many other unused learning support materials in the warehouse.

The inspection of the warehouse was premised on tips by ministry employees supporting the efforts of their now duly-mandated regional chief minister, Abdulraof Macacua, to fix the lingering adverse public perceptions about the MBHTE-BARMM.

Local executives in BARMM and officials of different parents-teachers associations in the region’s five provinces and three cities want the new MBHTE management to investigate why big volumes of textbooks, android tablets, computers and other learning devices the ministry had acquired long ago were never put to good use.
