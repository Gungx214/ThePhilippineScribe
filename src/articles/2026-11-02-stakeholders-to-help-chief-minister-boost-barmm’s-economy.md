**COTABATO CITY, Philippines**  --- Big business groups, peace-advocacy entities and the Moro National Liberation Front on Monday, November 2, separately committed support for the socio-economic objectives of the now duly-mandated Bangsamoro parliament, for them essential in harnessing the region's investment potentials.

The members of the 80-seat Bangsamoro parliament, elected during the region's September 14 parliamentary polls, held their inaugural session on Friday in the Bangsamoro regional capitol in Cotabato City.

Many communities in far-flung areas in the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao are still struggling to bounce back from underdevelopment caused by decades of secessionist strife, which waned only after the national government had forged separate peace compacts with the Moro National Liberation Front and, subsequently, with the Moro Islamic Liberation Front.

The lawyer-entrepreneur Ronald Hallid Torres, chairman of the influential Bangsamoro Business Council, most known as the BBC, and officials of their chapters in BARMM’s five provinces and three cities, separately told reporters on Monday that they are glad with the region's having a duly-mandated 80-member regional parliament now, whose chief minister is Abdulraof Macacua.

All of the interim parliament members, including Macacua, who served from 2019 until the September 14 BARMM regional elections, were presidential appointees. 

“Now we have an empowered parliament that can focus on legislations that can help us, in the business sector, make our economy grow to generate capital-intensive businesses, with capital inputs from local and foreign investors, which can provide employment for BARMM residents,” said Torres, also president of the multi-sector Regional Advisory Group of the Police Regional Office-Bangsamoro Autonomous Region.

Members of the PRO-BAR advisory group, among them lawyers from different BARMM ministries and support agencies, traditional community leaders and representatives from the Muslim and Christian religious communities and the academe, were elated with the peaceful transition on Friday from the interim regional parliament to one composed of duly-elected members.

Officials of different organizations of Chinese merchants in Cotabato City had also separately extended felicitations to Macacua for his nomination as chief minister of the now regular parliament.

The chairman of the MNLF, Muslimin Sema, whose tenure as labor and employment minister of BARMM under an interim parliament ended on Friday, said he and his companions in the front shall cooperate in maintaining law and order in their state-recognized bastions in support of Macacua’s peace and development agenda.

"We don’t have a problem doing that, all for an enduring peace in BARMM. We shall support the peace and development initiatives of our now regular parliament for our region's economy to flourish,” Sema, former regional labor and employment minister, said.

Sema, who had served as mayor of Cotabato City for three terms, said Macacua and his colleagues in the parliament can together work out the entry to BARMM of investors from member-states of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation, also known by its acronym, OIC.

The OIC, comprised of more than 50 Muslim states, including petroleum-exporting nations in the Middle East and North Africa, helped broker the 1996 government-MNLF final truce and, subsequently, the 2014 Comprehensive Agreement on Bangsamoro between the national government and the Moro Islamic Liberation Front, which paved the way for the replacement in 2019 of the then 27-year Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao with a more empowered BARMM.

Besides Sema, many other MNLF veterans, among them Hatimil Hassan, an erstwhile lawmaker in the now defunct interim BARMM parliament, Gerry Salapuddin, former governor and congressional representative in Basilan, and the former mayor of Omar town in Sulu, Hussein Mohammad, had also separately assured of their support for Macacua’s leadership in the Bangsamoro parliament.

Macacua was nominated chief minister of BARMM during the parliament’s inaugural session last Friday. As a duly-nominated chief minister, he shall also oversee the operations of all ministries and support agencies under the BARMM regional government.
