**COTABATO CITY** --- An attacker shot dead a popular Islamic missionary in Datu Paglas town in Maguindanao del Sur on Monday night, October 26, only about five hours after the fatal ambush of four Moro men in another area in the province.

The slain Ustadz Badruddin Bangkulit was known for his extensive involvement in community peacebuilding activities and in helping local executives resolve conflicts among Moro residents of Datu Paglas, one of the 22 towns in Maguindanao del Sur.

Officials of the Maguindanao del Sur Provincial Police Office and local executives in Datu Paglas told reporters on Tuesday that Bangkulit had just performed Aisha, the last of the five Islamic prayers for the day, and was whiling away time in their yard in Barangay Damawato in Datu Paglas when a man came close, pulled out a .45 caliber pistol and shot him repeatedly.

Bangkulit died from multiple bullet wounds sustained in the attack, whose perpetrator had escaped before responding barangay officials and volunteer community watchmen could reach the scene.

The incident was preceded by the ambush earlier on Monday of four men, the relatives Albasir Pamansag Tuang, Mohamad Tuang, Kenneth Tuang Mamalantong and Ogie Tuang in Sitio Ataw in Barangay Tulunan in Datu Anggal Midtimbang, Maguindanao del Sur.

Col. John Mangahis, Maguindanao del Sur’s provincial police director, said the four of them, residents of President Quirino town in Sultan Kudarat, died in the seats of their vehicle that gunmen shot with assault rifles.

Their attackers hurriedly ran away, leaving all of them lifeless.

Mangahis said the four men, riding a silver Toyota Vios, were on their way to an interior area in Sitio Ataw in Barangay Tulunan when they were waylaid by their assailants, whose identities investigators from the Datu Anggal Municipal Police Station and officials of Army units in the municipality are still trying to determine.

Mangahis said police investigators and local executives are cooperating in putting a closure to the incident.
