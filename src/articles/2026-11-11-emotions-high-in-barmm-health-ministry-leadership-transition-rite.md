COTABATO CITY  --- Hundreds of Bangsamoro health ministry employees bade their outgoing minister an emotional goodbye on Wednesday, November 11, and promised to support his successor in managing efficiently all of the medical service programs they have pioneered, as a big team, in recent years.

The physician-ophthalmologist Kadil Sinolinding, Jr., who started as regional health secretary of the now defunct Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao prior to his assumption as minister of the Ministry of Health in the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao, turned over his post to a successor, the lawyer Jay Salliman Datumanong during during a symbolic rite in Cotabato City on Wednesday morning.

The event, held at a function facility in uptown Cotabato City, was attended by regional health officials and newly-elected members of the BARMM parliament, among them Abrar Hataman and Haber Asarul. Salliman was also elected as a member of the 80-seat Bangsamoro parliament during the September 14 parliamentary polls in the autonomous region.

Three ranking MoH-BARMM physicians from different BARMM provinces, among them Mohammad Ariff Baguindali, director of the Bangsamoro Regional Hospital and Medical Center in Datu Hoffer town in Maguindanao del Sur, shed tears as they separately reported, during the event, the ministry’s accomplishment in their areas of responsibility as a result of their good working relations with Sinolinding.

Sinolinding and the remaining MoH-BARMM regional officials, including the physicians Abdulhalik Hashim, Zul Qarneyn Abas and Allen Minalang, who is chief of the Integrated Provincial Health Office-Lanao del Sur, took turns assuring Salliman of their support for his being the health ministry’s figurehead now.

The MoH-BARMM leadership transition rite was also witnessed by health workers from BARMM’s five provinces and the Bangsamoro Special Geographic Area, covering eight towns under the autonomous regional government, but are inside Cotabato, one of the four provinces in Administrative Region 12. 

Sinolinding, in a message, said he is certain that Salliman can efficiently sustain the medical service thrusts of the MoH-BARMM, which has hundreds of personnel deployed as field workers in each of the five provinces in the autonomous region.

“I will do my best to keep all of the programs of the ministry going. I am thankful for the commitment of support from all officials and personnel of the ministry to the assignments I am to do as health minister of the autonomous region,” Salliman said.

MoH-BARMM officials who attended the event talked about how they are to support Salliman’s being the region's health minister now.
