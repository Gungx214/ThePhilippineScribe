**COTABATO CITY** --- The media community in this city on Monday, November 23, commemorated the 17th anniversary of the “Maguindanao Massacre,” a politically-motivated carnage, in Ampatuan town in what is now Maguindanao del Sur, that left 58 people dead, 32 of them journalists.

The symbolic rite, organized by the Catholic Station DXMS here of the Oblates of Mary Immaculate congregation, was capped off with a candle lighting ceremony involving reporters from different broadcast outfits in this city and writers here of Manila-based media outfits.

Speakers then talked about their sad feelings on the incident, which shook the nation and the whole world to the core.

The massacre victims were on their way then to the Maguindanao provincial office of the Commission on Elections, to file the certificate of candidacy for governor of Ismael Mangudadatu, then mayor of Buluan town, when they were flagged down by members of the Ampatuan clan at a stretch of the Isulan-Cotabato Highway in Barangay Salman in Ampatuan, herded them to a nearby hinterland and killed them there, one after another, with pistols and assault rifles.

Mangudadatu was elected governor of the then still undivided Maguindanao province during the local elections a few months after the gruesome incident.

Managers of different radio stations in Cotabato City took turns urging, during their commemoration on Monday of the 17th anniversary of the Maguindanao Massacre, the local executives in what are now Maguindanao del Norte and Maguindanao del Sur provinces to respect press freedom and consider the Fourth Estate as a partner in fostering peace and sustainable development in municipalities under their administrations.

Drema Quitayen-Bravo and Erwin Cagampang Cabilbigan, managers of DXMS and the Radio Mindanao Network’s Station DXMY here, respectively, had pointed out in their messages, during their gathering on Monday afternoon, the need for Cotabato City-based journalists to cooperate in educating the public on the importance of press freedom in fostering peace and sustainable development in this city and in the nearby provinces of Maguindanao del Norte and Maguindanao del Sur.
