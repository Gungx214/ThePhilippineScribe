**COTABATO CITY** --- The Bangsamoro government will allocate P340 million for an extensive school meals program in 2027 covering all of the five provinces and three cities under its jurisdiction, regional officials said on Tuesday, November 24.

The lawyer Naguib Sinarimbo, education minister of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao, had told participants to the three-day 2nd Southeast Asia Regional Schools Meals Coalition Summit at the Westin Manila Hotel in Mandaluyong City, which started on Monday, that the BARMM government’s budget for its schools meals program for 2027 is ten times bigger than its fund allocation for the activity this year.

The central office of the Department of Education, the World Food Programme and the Schools Meals Coalition are together in the forefront of the November 23 to 25 2nd Southeast Asia Regional School Meals Coalition Summit at the Westin Manila Hotel.

The Schools Meals Coalition is a bloc of 113 countries that have feeding programs for children in schools. It was launched as a global network in 2021, following the United Nations’ Food System Summit in New York on September 23 in the same year.

Members of the 80-seat Bangsamoro parliament and officials under the office of the Bangsamoro chief minister, Abdulraof  Macacua, told reporters in Cotabato City on Tuesday that the BARMM government will expand in 2027 the schools meals program of the Ministry of Basic, Higher and Technical Education-BARMM. 

The office of Macacua and the MBHTE-BARMM shall together implement the program, according to regional officials.

Sinarimbo had also relayed to the summit attendees that there is also a strong collaboration, among schools in the autonomous region, different organizations of local farmers and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Agrarian Reform-BARMM and the Cooperative Social Enterprise Authority in providing school children in the autonomous region with healthy meals.

Sinarimbo had also reportedly told them that no fewer than 13,000 school children are benefitting from such an inter-agency, multi-sector feeding initiative.

Sinarimbo, elected as parliament member during the autonomous region’s September 14 first ever parliamentary elections, was appointed just two weeks ago as minister of the MBHTE-BARMM by Macacua.

Macacua, as figurehead of the 80-member BARMM lawmaking body, is also overseeing all ministries and support agencies under the Bangsamoro government.BARMM officials said Sinarimbo, while in Metro Manila, also met Education Secretary Sonny Angara, during which they talked about cooperation in regional education thrusts between the central office of the Department of Education and the MBHTE-BARMM.

Angara had reportedly assured Sinarimbo of DepEd’s support for capacity-building programs of the MBHTE-BARMM for its teachers and in setting up modular learning centers for the non-Moro indigenous people in the autonomous region.

Regional officials said Angara had also told Sinarimbo that the DepEd will also help push forward the efforts of the Bangsamoro government to boost the Madaris education in its provinces and cities, where there are Islamic schools being assisted by the MBHTE-BARMM.
