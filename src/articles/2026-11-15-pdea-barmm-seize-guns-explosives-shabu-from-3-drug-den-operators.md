**COTABATO CITY** --- Agents of the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency seized shabu, two assault rifles and explosives from three drug den operators entrapped on Saturday, November 14, in Barangay Gumaga in Libungan, Cotabato.

The three suspects, all reportedly linked to remnants of the now defunct Dawlah Islamiya terror group, are now in the custody of the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency-Bangsamoro Autonomous Region (PDEA-BARMM), to be prosecuted for violation of the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002.

George Paul Alcovindas, director of PDEA-BARMM, said on Sunday that their agents and policemen had seized from the three suspects P156,604 worth of shabu during a tradeoff right in their hideout in Sitio Purok 11 in Sitio Matibong in Barangay Gumaga in Libungan. 

The three male suspects collaborated in operating a drug den in the area, according to local executives, who helped the PDEA-BARMM and operatives from different units of the Police Regional Office 12 entrap them. 

Under state law, drug den operation is a non-bailable offense.

Local executives who supported the entrapment operation that resulted in the arrest of the three drug den operators had confirmed to reporters that PDEA-BARMM agents and policemen had also confiscated from them an M16 rifle, an M14 rifle, a shotgun, five rifle-propelled grenades and materials needed in assembling home-made bombs that can be detonated from a distance using mobile phones.

The three drug dealers arrested by PDEA-BARMM agents and and policemen on Saturday shared fractions of their earnings to remaining members of the now inactive Dawlah Islamiya terror group providing them sanctuary in hinterlands, according to local executives in Cotabato’s adjoining Libungan, Midsayap and Pigcawayan towns.
