**COTABATO CITY** --- The new minister of the Ministry of Basic, Higher and Technical Education-Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (MBHTE-BARMM) is now addressing alleged anomalies in its grant of scholarships and teaching positions to applicants in cities and provinces under its jurisdiction. 

The lawyer Naguib Sinarimbo, an elected member of the regional parliament and now BARMM education minister, said on Thursday, November 12, that he is now acting on reports to his office, from residents in the autonomous region, about the alleged wanton “sale” of vacant teaching positions in towns and cities in BARMM.

Sinarimbo assumed control of the MBHTE-BARMM only last week, following his election as member of the autonomous region’s 80-seat parliament during the first ever September 14 Bangsamoro parliamentary polls.

“Our chief minister, Abdulraof Macacua, wants us to immediately investigate and address the issues and concerns about our ministry that informants had relayed to us," Sinarimbo told reporters via text message on Thursday.

Sinarimbo had served as local government minister of BARMM prior to his stint as interim parliament member, along with 79 others, including Macacua, based on presidential appointments. Their tenures, as interim regional lawmakers, ended after the September 14 elections in BARMM. 

“The allegations are deeply alarming and, if proven true, constitute a grave betrayal of public trust. It is something that we have to focus our attention on,” Sinarimbo said.

He said his office is open to complainants who have issues against MBHTE-BARMM's operation modalities as an education ministry in the autonomous region.
