**COTABATO CITY** --- Two commuters died instantly while eight others were badly injured when the passenger van that they were riding together rammed a large Acacia tree along a stretch of a highway in Purok Maharlika in Barangay Saravia in Koronadal City, South Cotabato on Thursday night, November 26.

The rescuer-nurse Rolly Aquino, chief of the South Cotabato Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office, told reporters on Thursday that two passengers of the van, the relatives Sheila Mae Aliman, 36, and Meriam Samama Aliman, 65, died immediately from serious injuries caused by the accident.

Their cadavers had been turned over to their family for immediate burial according to Islamic tradition of burying the dead within 24 hours after death.

Eight others, including the driver of the van, who sustained fractures and wounds in different parts of their bodies, were rushed by emergency responders, from Aquino's office and personnel of the Bureau of Fire Protection in Koronadal City, to the South Cotabato Provincial Hospital for treatment.

Barangay officials in Saravia and investigators from the Koronadal City Police Station separately said on Friday that the speeding van veered towards one side of the highway when its driver lost control of the wheel, possibly due to mechanical trouble, and hit head-on a large Acacia tree. 

So strong was the impact of the collision that the passenger van was damaged beyond repair, according to Aquino.
