**COTABATO CITY** --- A group of soldiers in the Army’s 6th Infantry Division underwent an eight-day day training on emergency response and on-field first aid application and transport to hospitals of combatants wounded in action, or civilians injured in all sorts of accidents.

Radio reports on Sunday, November 1, stated that the Forward Treatment Unit Readiness Training in Camp Siongco in Datu Odin Sinsuat, Maguindanao del Norte for soldiers and Army reservists, which lasted from October 19 to 26, was handled by officials of the 6th ID’s Service Support Battalion, military physicians and emergency response experts from the Camp Siongco Station Hospital.

The Camp Siongco Station Hospital is inside Camp Siongco in Barangay Awang in Datu Odin Sinsuat, where the 6th ID’s headquarters is located.

Of the 62 soldiers who participated in the Forward Treatment Unit Readiness Training in Camp Siongco, 51 are personnel of different 6th ID units while 11 others are Army reservists from the 12th and 16th Regional Community Defense Groups.

Brig. Gen. Edgar Catu, commander of 6th ID, told reporters on Sunday that the eight-day training was facilitated by their Service Support Battalion, led by Lt. Col. Nonie Ruizal, assisted by two Army physicians, Col. Alexander Zapanta and Lt.Col. Jeremie Villamor.

Zapanta is the commanding officer in the Camp Siongco Station Hospital. Villamor is the executive officer of the hospital of the Army’s 10th Infantry Division in Camp General Manuel Yan, Sr. in Mawab town in Davao de Oro province.

“We appreciate a lot the efforts of the officers and their subordinates who cooperated in facilitating the Forward Treatment Unit Readiness Training in Camp Siongco,” Catu said. 

Lecturers taught trainees then on proper on-field care of civilian calamity, disaster and accident patients and soldiers wounded in gunfights.

The trainees also had extensive drills on facilitating airlift to hospital, or evacuation to safe areas, of patients using a Sikorsky UH-60 Black Hawk helicopter from the Tactical Operations Group 12 of the Philippine Air Force, based in the Cotabato Airport less than 500 meters away from the main entrance to Camp Siongco.

The training’s culmination rite was capped off with a simulated emergency response operation by participants inside Camp Siongco, held in the presence of Catu and Lt. Gen. Donald Gumiran, the commander of the Philippine Army.
