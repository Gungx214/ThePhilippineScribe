**COTABATO CITY** --- Five fuel retail stations in this city were asked by Mayor Bruce Matabalao to suspend operations, meantime, due to alleged non-compliance with petroleum products prices rollback, now being probed by his office. 

Chinese merchants and officials of big business blocs supporting Matabalao’s programs meant to boost local commerce and trade, among them members of the influential Bangsamoro Business Council that has chapters in the five provinces and three cities in the autonomous region, told reporters on Saturday, November 14, that they appreciate his immediate action on the issue. 

Matabalao’s administration is now verifying the alleged failure of the owners of the five fuel retail stations to comply with state petroleum prices rollback directives.

Radio reports had stated that the management of the five establishments ---the Ace Power, Apex Quality, El Depot, Petromart Gas Station and the Fuel Tank Shell Service Station --- had all reportedly agreed to cooperate with the city government’s investigation on the issue. 

Local executives said the probe is being initiated diplomatically, in the context of the authority of the city government based on the Local Government Code of 1991. 

Matabalao had ordered an extensive monitoring by his subordinates in the city government of the compliance by fuel retail stations, in all areas under its jurisdiction, with gasoline and diesel prices set by the national government.
