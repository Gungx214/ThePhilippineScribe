**COTABATO CITY** ---- There are six duly-elected members of the Bangsamoro parliament who are not newcomers to the complex world of regional governance and whose supporters who voted for them during the September 14 parliamentary polls are confident that they shall serve them well.

The most senior among the six of them, Datu Bimbo Sinsuat, started his public service career in the early 1980s, as one of the lawmakers in the now defunct Lupong Tagapagpaganap ng Pook 12 that covered all cities and provinces in Region 12, but not as empowered as what is now the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao.

A Lupong Tagapagpaganap ng Pook 9 also existed at that time, encompassing Region 9, including the island provinces of Basilan, Sulu and Tawi-Tawi. Both Basilan and Tawi-Tawi are now component-provinces of BARMM.

“I shall do my best to perform my duties as an elected member of the regional parliament,” Sinsuat said on Sunday.

A resident of Cotabato City, Sinsuat, whose hometown is Datu Odin Sinsuat in Maguindanao del Norte, had also served as member of the Regional Assembly of the now defunct Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao, or ARMM.

The ARMM was established in the early 1990s and got replaced with BARMM in 2019, a product of two decades of peace overtures between the national government and the southern Moro communities. 

Sinsuat is a known staunch supporter of the national government’s Mindanao peace process, partly aiming to foster peace and sustainable development in all areas affected by the secessionist conflicts during the 1970s.

Sinsuat and two other lawmakers who had also worked as ARMM officials, Amir Mawallil and Hanie Bud, all belong to the BARMM Government Coalition, or BGC, one of the more than ten parties that the Commission on Elections permitted to pit candidates during the September 14 regional elections. 

Mawallil was director of the Bureau of Public Information-ARMM for a number of years, until the creation of BARMM in 2019. Bud was a former regional ports manager of ARMM and had, subsequently, served as mayor of Maluso town in Basilan prior to his election as member of the parliament.

Sinsuat, Bud and Mawallil are together in the bloc representing the BGC Party to the 80-member BARMM lawmaking body.

Two lawyers, Ishak Mastura and Naguib Sinarimbo, also held regional posts in the ARMM while under different regional governors.

Mastura was a former regional secretary of the ARMM’s trade and investment department. He was also, for eight years, chairman of the ARMM’s Regional Board of Investments, now named Bangsamoro Regional Board of Investments under the BARMM government.

Sinarimbo was a former executive secretary of ARMM and became local government minister of BARMM, after its inception in 2019, until 2024.

Sinarimbo is the spokesperson of the Bangsamoro Federalist Party, whose president is the now duly-nominated chief minister of BARMM, Abdulraof Macacua, also an elected member of the parliament. Sinarimbo is the chairman of their party’s chapter in Cotabato City, where the 32-hectare BARMM government center is located.

Mastura, also from the Bangsamoro Federalist Party, and Sinarimbo and their now regular figurehead in the parliament, Macacua, were elected as representatives of parliamentary districts in Maguindanao del Norte province and in Cotabato City during the September 14 parliamentary polls in BARMM, whose territory encompasses five provinces, Maguindanao del Norte, Maguindanao del Sur, Lanao del Sur, Basilan and Tawi-Tawi and the cities of Lamitan, Marawi and Cotabato.

Another elected regional lawmaker, the physician Lampa Pandi, former mayor of Poona Bayabao in Lanao del Sur, had a long stint as regional health secretary of ARMM, during which he got involved in extensive medical missions in the region, which only had four provinces then, the still undivided Maguindanao, Lanao del Sur, Sulu and Tawi-Tawi.

Pandi, as municipal chief executive of Poona Bayabao, was also active in multi-sector peacebuilding activities in the barangays in the municipality, one of the 39 in Lanao del Sur, whose provincial capital is Marawi City.

Sinsuat, Bud, Mawallil, Mastura, Sinarimbo and Pandi separately told reporters on Sunday that they will support the peace and development initiatives of Macacua, who, as chief minister, shall also oversee all ministries and support agencies of the BARMM regional government.
